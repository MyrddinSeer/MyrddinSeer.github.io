Ozzie Battler Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Neutro B handles information in the Android game Ozzie Battler (package name com.neutrob.ozziebattler). It is written in plain English, with the Australian Privacy Principles in mind. It is not legal advice.

1. Who we are

Ozzie Battler is published by Neutro B, based in Australia.

  • Name: Neutro B
  • Country: Australia

2. What this app collects

The app does not collect personal information from you, and it does not send game data to us or to anyone else.

Ozzie Battler is a free game. At the date of this policy it has:

  • no advertisements
  • no in-app purchases
  • no user accounts or sign-in
  • no in-game chat or online play
  • no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or Google Play Games software in the app

We do not sell personal information. We do not share game data with third parties, because the app does not send that data off the device.

3. What stays on your device

The game stores progress and preferences only in the app’s private storage on your device. This data does not leave the device through the app. It may include:

  • game settings (for example sound and music, difficulty, turn timer, and similar options)
  • local player profiles, including a short display name and avatar you choose (up to six profiles)
  • on-device game statistics and a match log (for example wins, favourite characters, and recent match results)
  • an optional statistics file you can export, which is also written only to the app’s storage on your device

A display name is optional in the sense that you choose what to type. Please do not enter information you would not want stored on the device. These names and stats stay on the device and are not sent to us.

4. Permissions

The current Google Play release of Ozzie Battler does not declare any Android permissions, including the INTERNET permission. The game cannot open a network connection to send or receive data.

The app is also set so that Android’s automatic backup does not copy its files to cloud backup.

5. Third parties, including Google Play

We do not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or Google Play Games services in the app.

If you download, install, or update Ozzie Battler from Google Play, Google may collect information about that activity — for example your Google account, device information, and install or usage figures — under Google’s Privacy Policy. That collection is by Google, not by us. The app itself does not send us a list of who has played.

If you email us, we will use your email address and the content of your message only to reply and to keep a record of the enquiry.

6. Children

Ozzie Battler may be played by children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It does not have social features, accounts, advertisements, or online play.

A parent or guardian can remove local game data by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. Individual local profiles can also be deleted inside the game.

The answers given in Google Play about the app’s target audience and any Play Families declarations must match this description. Those answers are set separately in Google Play Console.

7. Retention and deletion

Because we do not collect game data, we do not keep a copy of your settings, profiles, or match history on our systems.

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • delete a local profile inside the game
  • clear the app’s storage in your device settings, or
  • uninstall the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the local save files described above.

Email you send us is kept only for as long as it is needed to respond and to maintain a simple record of the enquiry.

8. Security

The app has no internet permission, so it does not transmit your game data over the internet. Local files are kept in the app’s private storage on your device.

No device or method of storage is perfectly secure. We recommend that you use a screen lock on your device.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. If we later add online features, advertisements, in-app purchases, accounts, or any practice that collects personal information, we will update this policy before that version is released, and we will review the Google Play Data safety form at the same time.
