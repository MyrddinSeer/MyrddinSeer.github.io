About Neutro B

Neutro B is an independent game and story studio.

The first game is Ozzie Battler, an Android game coming to Google Play. Stories and updates are also shared on the Neutro B YouTube channel.

Ozzie Battler

Ozzie Battler is Neutro B’s first game. It is a free, offline Android card battler, played against the built-in AI or passed between two players on the same device. It has no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no user accounts, and no online play.

The game is coming to Google Play. The Ozzie Battler privacy policy is on this site.
